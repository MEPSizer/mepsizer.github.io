Open new MEPtools

Choose your device

1Install the new MEPtools

  1. Tap the Open new MEPtools button above (it opens in Chrome).
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top-right of Chrome.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Tap Install. The new MEPtools icon appears on your home screen.

2Uninstall the old MEPtools

  1. Find the old MEPtools icon on your home screen or app list.
  2. Touch and hold the icon.
  3. Tap Uninstall (or App info → Uninstall).
  4. Tap OK to confirm.
Not sure which icon is old? Open it. If it shows this "App moved" page, it is the old one.

1Install the new MEPtools

  1. Open https://meptools.github.io/ in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an up arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The new MEPtools icon appears on your home screen.

2Remove the old MEPtools

  1. Find the old MEPtools icon on your home screen.
  2. Touch and hold the icon.
  3. Tap Remove App, then Delete from Home Screen (or Delete).
Not sure which icon is old? Open it. If it shows this "App moved" page, it is the old one.

1Install the new MEPtools

  1. Open https://meptools.github.io/ in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Chrome: click the install icon at the right side of the address bar, or ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app.
  3. Edge: click … → Apps → Install this site as an app.
  4. Click Install. MEPtools opens in its own window and gets a desktop / Start menu icon.

2Uninstall the old MEPtools

  1. Open the old MEPtools app window (it will show this "App moved" page).
  2. Click the ⋮ or … menu at the top of the app window.
  3. Click Uninstall MEPtools, then Remove.
Windows: you can also go to Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find the old MEPtools and click Uninstall.
