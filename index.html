About MEPtools

Duct sizing calculator for everyday MEP work. Works offline once installed. Nothing you type leaves your device.

  • Equal-friction / velocity sizing: enter any two of flow rate, head loss, velocity and equivalent diameter.
  • Method: Darcy-Weisbach with Colebrook-White friction factor; target head loss is solved with the Swamee-Jain explicit form, then the result is checked with Colebrook-White. Roughness 0.0007 ft (0.213 mm).
  • Rectangular ducts: converted to the equivalent round duct for the same friction and airflow (Huebscher, as in ASHRAE).
  • Pipe Slope: enter the start IL and the distance from the previous point; each new point only needs its distance. Slope ratio (default 1:120) and direction (Up to Down / Down to Up) apply to the whole table.
  • Rain Water Calci: Q uses the correct Metric or IP formula; RWO is based on 272 m² (approximately 2,927.78 ft²) per outlet as a planning reference.
  • Air: standard air and four humid-air conditions (Metric and IP each have their own list).
  • Head loss is on the standard-air chart basis; velocity pressure and Reynolds number use the selected air.

Results were checked against reference duct-sizer values (within about 0.05%). Always confirm against your project standards before issuing drawings.
